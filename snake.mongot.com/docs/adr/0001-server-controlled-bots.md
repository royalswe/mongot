# Server-controlled bots

Bots are server-side room participants and use the same seats, readiness, movement simulation, and collision rules as humans. Their bounded beam search prioritizes survival depth, coupled with multi-agent Voronoi territory partitioning, dead-end pocket avoidance, boundary wall-hugging compactness, and aggressive cutoff intercepts that box active opponents against boundaries and walls while actively dodging head-on collisions; all match participants' occupied cells—including eliminated players' retained bodies—remain blocked, and each decision inspects at most 10,000 cells. Keeping decisions server-side preserves authoritative outcomes and avoids trusting client movement, while the fixed budget limits added work under load.
