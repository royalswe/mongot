# Server-controlled bots

Bots are server-side room participants and use the same seats, readiness, movement simulation, and collision rules as humans. Their bounded beam search prioritizes survival depth, then pursues safe intercepts by projecting active opponents briefly along their current direction; all match participants' occupied cells—including eliminated players' retained bodies—remain blocked, and each decision inspects at most 10,000 cells. Keeping decisions server-side preserves authoritative outcomes and avoids trusting client movement, while the fixed budget limits added work under load.
